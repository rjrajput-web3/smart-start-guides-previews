# 3-Sentence Outreach Template (free preview — fill from YOUR verified lead)

Sentence 1 — observation (public milestone/infrastructure from the posting):
> I saw [Company]'s [date] posting for [exact role title] to [what the
> posting says the role does, in its own words].

Sentence 2 — problem + operational impact (use the posting's own targets):
> Hitting [day-30/60/90 target from the posting] leaves almost no slack
> for [the bottleneck the role exists to fix], so [concrete risk if it
> slips].

Sentence 3 — low-friction CTA (one sample, no meeting ask, no sales jargon):
> Would a one-page sample [deliverable in the posting's format], done
> [the way the posting describes], be useful to judge the fit?

Rules: send only via the route the posting names (application form or a
publicly listed company channel). Never send to a guessed email address.
Never quote a rate the posting does not publish. Never invent a stat,
client, or sample — invented claims are an explicit disqualifier in most
AI-native postings.
